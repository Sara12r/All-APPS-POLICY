Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Love Stickers ("we", "our", "the app") is a sticker application for Android. This policy explains what information the app handles. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or share any personal information on our servers. The app works on your device:

  • Stickers are rendered and stored only on your device.
  • Your country selection, favorites, downloads and reward credits are saved locally on your device and are never uploaded by us.
  • The app has no login, no account, and no analytics of your personal chats.

2. Third-Party Services

The app displays advertisements via Google AdMob (Google LLC). AdMob may automatically collect certain information to serve ads, such as:

  • Device identifiers (e.g., Advertising ID)
  • Approximate location (region/city level, derived from IP)
  • Ad interaction data (views, clicks)
  • Device model, operating system version

Google's use of this data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

You can opt out of personalized advertising in your device: Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID / Opt out of Ads Personalization.

3. WhatsApp Integration

The app lets you add sticker packs to WhatsApp using WhatsApp's official third-party sticker API. When you add or send stickers, WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies to that data. We do not receive any information about your WhatsApp chats.

4. Internet & Content

The app downloads sticker packs (image files) from our content storage (Cloudflare R2). These requests contain no personal data — only the image file names. Downloaded stickers and saved images are stored in your device gallery (Pictures/Love Stickers) and remain under your control.

5. Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

6. Permissions

  • Internet — to load sticker content and advertisements.
  • Network state — to check connectivity before loading ads/content.

No camera, contacts, location, microphone or storage permissions are required.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
