Privacy Policy

App: Arrow Escape
Last updated: October 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Arrow Escape ("we", "our", "the App") handles information when you use our mobile application. By using the App, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information We Collect

The App is designed to work entirely offline on your device.

  • Game progress (unlocked levels, cleared levels, stars, hints, themes, sound preferences, and your chosen nickname) is stored only in a local database on your device. This data never leaves your phone and is never uploaded to any server.
  • We do not require an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is supported by advertisements delivered through Google AdMob, a service provided by Google LLC.

  • AdMob may collect and use certain information automatically, such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, approximate (non-precise) location, device model, operating system, and how you interact with ads, in order to show you relevant ads.
  • Google's use of advertising data is governed by Google's Privacy & Terms.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising in your device settings: Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID / Opt out of ads personalization.
  • The App may show interstitial ads, banner ads, native ads, and rewarded video ads. Rewarded ads are shown only when you choose to watch them in exchange for in-game rewards (such as extra hints or continuing a level).

3. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the App does not collect personal information from any user, no such data is stored or shared. If you are a parent or guardian and have concerns, please contact us.

4. Data Storage & Retention

  • All game data is saved locally on your device using the App's internal database.
  • If you uninstall the App, or clear its data from your device settings, all locally stored game progress will be permanently deleted.
  • We have no access to, and no backup of, your locally stored game data.

5. Permissions

The App requests only the following permissions, which are required for ads to work:

  • Internet (android.permission.INTERNET) — to load advertisements.
  • Network state (android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) — to check whether a network connection is available before loading ads.

6. Third-Party Services

The only third-party service used in the App is Google AdMob (advertising). We do not use analytics SDKs, crash-reporting SDKs, cloud storage, or any social login. Third-party ad networks may display their own content and collect data under their own privacy policies.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
