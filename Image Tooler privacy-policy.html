Privacy Policy

Photo Tools: Compress, Resize & PDF

Last updated: 25 October 2026

1 Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Blackwon ("we", "us") handles information when you use the Android application Photo Tools: Compress, Resize & PDF (package: com.photocompress.resizer.jpgtopng.imagetopdf.kb) — the photo compressor, resizer, cropper, format converter, and image-to-PDF toolkit.

🔒 Core design principle: your photos are processed entirely on your device. The app works without an account and never requires you to sign in.

2 Your Photos and Files

  • Photos you pick are processed locally on your device. They are never uploaded to any server controlled by us.
  • When you use a tool, the result is written as a new file in your device gallery — images go to the Pictures/Photo Tools folder and PDFs go to the Downloads/Photo Tools folder.
  • Your original photos are never modified, deleted, or moved by the app.
  • The app does not read, scan, or index photos you have not explicitly selected.

3 Permissions

  • Photo Picker: The app uses Android's system Photo Picker so you can choose exactly which images the app may access. The app does not request broad storage access.
  • Saving files: Results are saved through the standard Android gallery (MediaStore) system — no extra permissions are required on supported Android versions.
  • The app does not access your contacts, camera, microphone, or precise location.

4 Advertising

The app displays ads through Google AdMob (Google LLC). Google and its partners may collect identifiers and usage data to serve and measure ads, and where required by law you will be asked for consent before ads are personalized.

You can learn more here:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses data from apps using its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Where applicable, you can change or withdraw your ad personalization choice anytime from the app's consent prompt or your Android settings.

5 Data We Collect

  • By the app itself: None of your personal data is collected, transmitted, or sold by the app. No account is required.
  • By the ad SDK: Google's ad SDK may collect data as described in Section 4 in order to serve ads. Please review Google's privacy policy for details.
  • Local settings: Your theme preference, launch counter, and rating-prompt status are stored only on your device.

6 Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will take appropriate action.

7 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
